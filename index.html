Why write in two languages为什么用两种语言写作

The premise 出发点

A translation is not an attachment you bolt on at the end. It is the second reading of your paper — the one many of your readers will actually use.

译文不是最后才补上的附件，
而是论文的第二种读法——
读者真正会去读的那一种。

BiWrite keeps both readings alive while you write.

BiWrite 让两种语言在你写作的时候同时成立。

  1. 01

    Open a paper

    打开论文

    A .tex, .md or .txt file. If you already keep a Chinese version, pair it and BiWrite uses it.

    支持 .tex、.md、.txt。已经有中文版就配对进来，BiWrite 直接使用你的译文。

  2. 02

    Write once

    只写一遍

    One side is yours to edit. The other follows paragraph by paragraph, revising only what changed.

    一侧由你编辑，另一侧逐段跟随；只修订改动过的段落，其余一字不动。

  3. 03

    Finish in both

    双语定稿

    Compile the PDF beside you, polish with rules, and export the Chinese version when you need it.

    PDF 在旁边编译，按规则润色，需要时导出中文版本。

01

Bilingual editing双语编辑

The other language keeps up with you.

另一种语言，
跟着你的笔走。

English on the left, Chinese on the right — or the other way round. Stop typing for a moment and the paragraph you changed is translated again, revised from its previous translation. Nothing else is touched.

左侧英文，右侧中文，也可以反过来。停下打字，改过的段落就会以原译文为基础重新修订；其余段落，一字不动。

  • ¶

    Only edited paragraphs. A changed paragraph is revised from its old translation, not translated from scratch, and the result streams in.

    只重译改过的段落。以原译文为基础修订，而不是推倒重来；结果以流式返回。

  • ⟦⟧

    Placeholders, both ways. Math, citations, cross-references, labels, URLs and comments reach the model as placeholders and return byte for byte.

    占位符来回无损。公式、引用、交叉引用、标签、网址和注释以占位符发给模型，返回后逐字节还原。

  • ↔

    Switch or pair. EN ↔ 中 puts Chinese on the left to edit. Pair paper.tex with paper_zh.tex and your own Chinese is used — with no translation requests.

    换边或配对。用 EN ↔ 中 把中文放到左侧编辑。把 paper.tex 与 paper_zh.tex 配对，你的中文直接作为译文——不产生翻译请求。

02

LaTeX, in placeLaTeX，原地编译

The PDF sits beside the source.

PDF 就在
稿纸旁边。

Compile with your own TeX distribution. Unsaved edits compile in a hidden cache, so the file on disk stays exactly as you last saved it. Click a sentence in the PDF and its source is selected.

用你本机的 TeX 发行版编译。未保存的修改在缓存里编译，磁盘上的 .tex 保持你上次保存的样子。点 PDF 里的句子，源码中的那一句随即被选中。

  • ⌘B

    Unsaved, still compiled. Edits are written to a .biwrite/ cache folder; your paper on disk does not move.

    不保存也能编译。修改写入 .biwrite/ 缓存文件夹，磁盘上的论文保持原样。

  • ↵

    Click to source. A click on a PDF sentence selects it in the source through SyncTeX. ⌘⌥J finds the cursor's paragraph in the PDF.

    点 PDF，回到源码。由 SyncTeX 定位到对应句子；⌘⌥J 在 PDF 中高亮光标所在的段落。

  • 中

    A Chinese PDF too. XeLaTeX and ctex build a PDF from the translation; the Chinese .tex can be exported on its own.

    还有中文 PDF。用 XeLaTeX 和 ctex 由译文编译，也可以单独导出中文 .tex。

  • TeX

    Any engine, real templates. latexmk, pdfLaTeX, XeLaTeX or LuaLaTeX. PVLDB 2027, ICLR 2027 and IEEE TII are built in.

    各种引擎，真实模板。latexmk、pdfLaTeX、XeLaTeX、LuaLaTeX。内置 PVLDB 2027、ICLR 2027 和 IEEE TII。

03

Writing assistant写作助手

A second reader who knows the rules.

多一位懂规矩的读者。

Select a sentence and ask. Polish it, question it, write the next paragraph in the manner of your references, draw the figure, or repair the LaTeX. Jobs run in the background and results arrive as diffs; nothing enters the paper until you accept it.

选中一句，然后发问。润色、提问、按参照样例仿写下一段、画图、修 LaTeX。任务在后台运行，结果以差异显示；你点采纳，它才进入论文。

In a LaTeX paper, Accept first has the model fit the result to your document, then compiles it. If it needs a package or a TikZ library, the model repairs that before the text is written.

在 LaTeX 论文中，采纳会先让模型把结果改成适合这篇文档的样子，再编译一次。缺宏包或缺 TikZ 库时，模型会先修好，再写入正文。

Wait as long as you like. Edits you make while a job runs are not overwritten — the result waits for you.

可以慢慢等。任务运行期间你继续写的内容不会被覆盖，结果会放在那里等你。

Polish润色

Rewrite under the writing rules, or under your own instruction. Claims, numbers, citations and formulas stay fixed.

按写作规则改写，也可以写上你的要求。论断、数字、引用和公式保持不变。

Ask提问

Questions about the selection or the whole paper, with follow-ups. Answers, never edits.

就所选文字或整篇论文回答，可以追问。只给回答，不改正文。

Imitate仿写

A new paragraph after the cursor, in the voice of your writing samples, with a facing Chinese version.

在光标后写新段落，模仿你写作样例的文风，并附对照中文。

Figures图表

LaTeX code, a compiled PDF, or an editable PowerPoint diagram — traced from a draft or only in its style.

LaTeX 代码、编译好的 PDF，或可编辑的 PowerPoint 示意图——可以临摹，也可以只仿风格。

Fix语法修复

TeX errors handed to the model with their lines, up to three rounds, shown as a diff before anything changes.

把 TeX 错误连同所在行交给模型，最多三轮；改动以差异显示，你确认后才写入。

Via translation按译文改

Rewrite the Chinese; the English follows with the smallest edit that means the same thing.

改写中文，英文以最小的改动成为同一个意思。

Does it work offline?可以离线使用吗？

Opening, writing, compiling and paired translations work offline. New translations and assistant jobs need your model endpoint, which can be a local server such as Ollama.

打开、写作、编译和已有的配对译文都可以离线完成。新的翻译和助手任务需要模型接口，也可以接本地的 Ollama。

What happens to my files and keys?文件和密钥会被怎样处理？

Your files stay where they are; edits are only written when you save. Keys go into the system keychain and are never shown again. Request logs stay on this machine and do not record keys.

文件留在原来的位置，只有保存时才写入。密钥保存在系统钥匙串中，之后不再显示。请求日志只存在本机，不记录密钥。

Can I keep my existing Chinese version?可以继续用我已有的中文版吗？

Yes. Pair paper.tex with paper_zh.tex, or sections_en/ with sections_zh/. BiWrite matches paragraphs by citations, labels, formulas and numbers, and writes each edited paragraph back in place.

可以。把 paper.tex 与 paper_zh.tex 配对，或 sections_en/ 与 sections_zh/。BiWrite 按引用、标签、公式和数字匹配段落，保存时把每个改动过的段落原位写回。
